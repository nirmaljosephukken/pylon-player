Pylon is free and open source

A calmer media player,
built for watching shows.

Pylon runs on VLC's engine, so it plays everything VLC plays. On top sits a quieter interface and the small things that make a season nicer to watch: presets per show, subtitles you can drag into sync, and a queue that remembers where you were.

Getting started

Three steps, about two minutes

Pylon uses the VLC engine that's already on your computer instead of shipping its own copy. That keeps the download small and means you get VLC's format support and hardware decoding.

Install VLC

Get the 64-bit version from videolan.org. Skip this if you already have it.

Install Pylon

Run the setup file. It installs just for you, no admin needed. If SmartScreen appears, click More info then Run anyway.

Open a show

Drop a file or a whole season folder on the window. Pylon keeps itself up to date from then on.

Install VLC

From your package manager, for example sudo apt install vlc or sudo dnf install vlc.

Run the AppImage

Make it executable with chmod +x Pylon-*.AppImage, then double-click it or run it from a terminal.

Open a show

Drop a file or a whole season folder on the window and press play.

Install VLC

Download it from videolan.org and drag it into Applications.

Open Pylon

Unzip and move Pylon to Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open, because the build isn't signed by Apple yet.

Open a show

Drop a file or folder on the window. The Mac build is experimental, so reports are very welcome.

Features

Small things that add up

Everything you'd expect from a player, plus a few ideas of its own.

Subtitles you can actually fix

Out of sync? Pause when someone starts talking and drag the subtitle timeline until their line sits under the playhead. No guessing at milliseconds.

  • Add subtitles to a whole season at once
  • Font, size, colour, outline and position
  • Find subtitles online in one click

See where you're jumping

Hover the seek bar for a preview picture of that moment. Click once and you're there, with no flicker.

  • Controls fade away when you stop moving the mouse
  • Bookmarks shown right on the bar
  • Picture-in-picture mini player

Your music, too

A music library that reads your folders and shows artists, albums and songs with their cover art.

Presets per show

Save subtitle timing, picture and speed for a show. Every episode opens just right.

Continue watching

Pick up where you left off. Playlists resume at the first episode you haven't seen.

Mark as watched

Tick off episodes yourself, or let Pylon do it as you finish them.

A smarter queue

Drag to reorder, shuffle, save as a playlist, or play straight from a folder.

One-click updates

Pylon checks once a day and installs new versions in place. Your settings stay.

Plays everything

MKV, MP4, AVI, network streams and more, because VLC's engine does the playing.

Themes

Eight looks, four of them neon

Switch any time from Preferences. You can also pick your own accent colour.

Questions

Good to know

Why do I need VLC installed?

Pylon is a new interface on top of VLC's playback engine. Using the copy on your computer keeps Pylon small and gives you VLC's format support, hardware decoding and its own security updates.

Is it free? Is there tracking?

Free and open source under the MIT licence. No accounts and no analytics. The only thing Pylon connects to by itself is GitHub, once a day, to check for updates, and you can turn that off in Preferences.

Is this an official VLC app?

No. Pylon is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by VideoLAN.

Something broke, or I have an idea

Please open an issue on GitHub. Pylon is still a beta, and most fixes ship within a day or two.
